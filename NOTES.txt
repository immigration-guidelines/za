South Africa - published guidance for officials deciding visa, residence and asylum cases
Checked 2026-11-15. Files are fetched by ../download_za.py (python3 download_za.py).

Result in one sentence
The Department of Home Affairs (DHA) publishes a thin, unsystematic selection of its
Immigration Directives and nothing else: no handbook, no standard operating procedure,
no register of directives. 38 documents were saved (2019 to July 2026); 35 are true
guidance, but most of them are short concessions, waivers and exemption extensions
rather than instructions on how to assess a case.

What Immigration Directives are
Numbered instructions signed by the Minister, the Director-General or the Deputy
Director-General: Immigration Services, addressed to "all officials of the department",
head office, ports of entry, missions abroad and the visa facilitation centres (VFS).
Numbers restart each year and run to at least 24 a year (No. 24 of 2019, No. 22 of
2025), so the department issues some dozens a year. Two parallel series exist
("Immigration Directive No. x" and "Minister's Immigration Directive No. x"), which is
why two different documents can both be "No. 1 of 2022". Only the ones the department
wants the public to know about are put on its site, under "Notices", as loose PDF files,
as images (PNG/JPG scans) or as the text of a web page. Some Minister's directives on
exemption permits are also printed in the Government Gazette.

What is in this folder (see index.tsv; kind and official columns)
- guidance, official (34): Immigration Directives, Minister's Immigration Directives,
  two Director-General circulars, ministerial waiver letters and one adjudication
  spreadsheet tool. Of these:
    about 8 tell officials how to handle or assess cases (critical skills list
      implementation 2022, twice; points-based system 2024 with its waiver letters and
      adjudication spreadsheet; port-of-entry visa requirements for missions 2024;
      detention forms and procedure after the Constitutional Court order 2024);
    about 26 are concessions, waivers, withdrawals and extensions of exemption permits
      (Zimbabwean and Lesotho exemption permits, visa backlog concessions, Covid-19
      measures) that bind officials but say little about decision-making.
- guidance, mirror (1): the original Immigration Directive No. 22 of 2025 of 23
  September 2025, saved from the Law Society of South Africa's site; the department's
  site carried only the amended version of 30 September 2025 (also saved, official).
- procedural act, official (3): the gazetted points-based system notice of 9 October
  2024 and two notice texts of October 2024 (withdrawal and new criteria) as the
  department published them. They set the criteria adjudicators apply.
Formats: 30 PDF (many are scans without a text layer), 6 images, 1 Excel file, 1 web
page. Files whose date is known only to the month have a YYYY-MM prefix; dates of
scanned documents were read from the page and two handwritten ones are uncertain (said
in the title).

Where the copies come from
- www.dha.gov.za answers robots.txt with HTTP 403 to scripts, so the live site was not
  used at all. All 33 DHA files were taken from the Internet Archive's captures of that
  site (web.archive.org); they are the department's own files and are marked official =
  yes, with the capture address in index.tsv. The list of what exists was built from the
  archive's index of dha.gov.za (about 800 PDF addresses and the "Notices" pages).
- www.gov.za (robots.txt allows the gazette files): 4 gazetted Minister's directives
  (No. 15 of 2024, Nos. 20 and 21 of 2025, No. 10 of 2026).
- www.lssa.org.za (robots.txt allows everything): 1 mirror copy.

Sources checked that yielded nothing more
- DHA site sections seen through the archive: Notices, Immigration Services (types of
  visas, permanent residency, refugee status and asylum, Standing Committee for Refugee
  Affairs, overstay and prohibition appeals), Statements and speeches, the /images/
  PDFs, notices, gazettes, ZEP, LSP and Documents folders. Apart from the directives,
  they hold legislation, gazette notices, forms, applicant checklists and requirement
  lists (left out as applicant information), policy papers, tenders and vacancies.
- No standard operating procedure for refugee reception offices, refugee status
  determination, the Standing Committee for Refugee Affairs or the Refugee Appeals
  Authority is published. A Director-General's directive of 28 September 2026 to all
  refugee reception offices on taking in asylum applications is known only from the
  statement of 9 October 2026 announcing its withdrawal.
- Government Printing Works (www.gpwonline.co.za): no robots.txt (404); not needed,
  since www.gov.za carries the same gazettes.
- Unofficial copies: law firms, advisers and universities republish individual
  directives. They were used only for the one item above. Stellenbosch University's file
  server answers robots.txt with HTTP 403 and was not used.

Refusals met
- www.dha.gov.za: HTTP 403 on robots.txt. Host not used; the script checks robots.txt
  once per run and uses the live site only if that ever changes.
- web.archive.org: no robots.txt (404). It throttled this machine throughout, refusing
  the connection after roughly every ten requests. Requests were spaced 8 to 20 seconds
  apart and each run stopped after three refusals in a row. One item is still missing
  for this reason (see not-obtained.tsv); re-running the script later should fetch it.
- files.su.ac.za: HTTP 403 on robots.txt; not used.
- webarchive.archive.unhcr.org and www.refworld.org: HTTP 403 (bot check); not used.

Completeness
- The collection is what the department chose to publish and the Internet Archive
  happened to capture. Directives published only briefly, or before about 2019, are
  missing. Numbers cited in saved documents but not found are listed in not-obtained.tsv.
- Seven gazette files in the department's folders were not opened, to spare the archive;
  they may include further gazetted directives. Two notice pages (2020 and 2022) could
  not be read because the archive refused the connection.
- Because the live site could not be read, anything published on it since the archive's
  last captures (September to October 2026) is not covered.

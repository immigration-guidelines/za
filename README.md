# Immigration guidance: South Africa

Published guidance that the authorities of South Africa issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 41 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

South Africa publishes no procedures manual and no register of its directives. This repository holds 38 documents from 2019 to 2026 that the Department of Home Affairs did publish: Immigration Directives, Minister's directives, Director-General circulars, waiver letters and the points-based adjudication spreadsheet. About eight say how to assess cases (the critical skills list, the points-based system, port-of-entry visa requirements, the detention procedure after the Constitutional Court order); the rest are concessions, waivers and extensions of exemption permits. Numbering shows at least 24 directives a year, of which only a handful are published. The department's live site refuses automated access, so almost all files are Internet Archive copies of it; `index.tsv` says which are official copies and which one is a mirror.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
